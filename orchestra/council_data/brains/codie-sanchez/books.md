# Books: Codie Sanchez

Two books. Built from how she teaches them in interviews, her own summary video, and her signed newsletter pieces; book text was not fetched. Summaries are in my own words.

## Main Street Millionaire (2024)
- **Core thesis:** Wealth comes from owning equity, not from a salary, and ordinary people can own it by buying small, old, simple, profitable Main Street businesses. Private equity's acquisition process works the same at a small scale, and buyers can use other people's money and creative terms instead of their own savings. It is meant as a short, plain M&A education without billion-dollar jargon.
- **Key frameworks:** the ten steps to buy a business (realize the opportunity, deal clarity, origination, financing, selling the owner on you, diligence, negotiation, structure and documents, closing, then the first 90 days and year), with the book grouped under research, invest, command, and harness; the four levels of business buying (solo venture, hands-on CEO, on-deck operator, market leader); the deal box and deal clarity worksheet; the stale, old, weak, simple screen and the Lindy effect; the 100, 50, 10, 1 funnel; the seven Ds of why owners sell; the four A's for off-market deals (accountants, attorneys, insurance agents, advisors); 21 creative financing methods, led by seller financing pitched as more money, lower taxes, and a faster close; 12 ways to find a business; price versus terms (chapter eight); a 90-day takeover that changes little in the first 60 days; cash flow habits (weekly finance reviews, the KO process of cut, automate, delegate, outsource, three-tier pricing); and building toward a sellable asset from the day you buy. It ships with calculators, templates, and a list of about 130 businesses to buy.
- **Context:** She says it took three years to write and is short on purpose (S001). Released December 2024 (S014); she reports it reached the New York Times bestseller list in late 2024 (S028). Her timeline for a first deal shifted between tellings: 3, 6, and 12 months in 2023 (S027) and 3, 9, and 12 months in the 2024 summary (S023).
- **Taught in sources:** her own summary video (S023); interviews and talks around the launch (S001, S007, S011, S012, S014, S018); the annual letter (S028). The same frameworks run through her deal-sourcing and financing playbooks (S020, S021, S022).

## Own or Be Owned (2026)
- **Core thesis:** Most owners are owned by their business: it runs on them, so they are self-employed with a bigger job. The fix is to run the business the way private equity runs a portfolio company, with a diagnosis, a score, a strategy, and quarterly reviews, and to know your own owner type so you can see where you will win and fail. The goal is a business so good it does not need you.
- **Key frameworks:** the Owner Score, a short assessment that sorts owners into 12 archetypes such as the closer, ball hog, visionary, artist, martyr, and workhorse; business-or-job tests (the two-week vacation test, key man risk, and whether anyone inside is better than you); start with the P&L to find where money is hiding, then pricing and payment terms, then a growth roadmap; the 12 profit levers, with pricing, product, persona, profit, process, and people as the core and Promote as the advertising lever (a full chapter on ads, the Ad Trap, and the Rent Check); lifestyle, scale, or exit, chosen for a year at a time; the hero and martyr traps; the entrepreneur pyramid from doer to CEO; two top metrics per business unit; the hiring matrix and incentive levers; a proof vault in the chapter on pitch; and the Bottleneck Question about what system must exist so it does not matter who leaves. Each chapter has a part for owners and a part for employees.
- **Context:** She says she built it after running close to 5,000 people through the system over five years (S016); her July 2026 newsletter says over 14,000 owners (S031). It launched September 18, 2026 with a live giveaway of $1M in cash and prizes (S031, S002). In December 2024 she described a planned next book as an operating system for scaling from six to nine figures (S014); this is most likely the same book (inferred from S004, S014, S016).
- **Taught in sources:** S002, S004, S016, S031, S041. Related lessons on being needed versus being valuable appear in S035.

## Books she sends people to (not hers)
- Economic Facts and Fallacies by Thomas Sowell, for understanding numbers instead of repeating them (S008).
- Never Split the Difference by Chris Voss, for small-business negotiation (S021, S023).
- Adventure Capitalist by Jim Rogers, which she says most shaped her career (S019).
- The 4-Hour Workweek by Tim Ferriss, for cutting busywork (S019).
- Die with Zero by her mentor Bill Perkins (S008, S015).
- Buffett's latest letter, which she rereads before she invests (S003).
- She prefers biographies of people who did it over self-help: "I'd rather read 10 uh billionaires biographies than 200 self-help books because they've actually done it" (S001)
